# Disaster recovery and migration

Three guide pages cover what to back up ahead of time, how to recover funds after a catastrophic failure of an [[lnd]] node, and how to move a healthy node to a new machine. The guides treat recovery (unscheduled, e.g. hardware failure) and migration (scheduled move) as different processes ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/recovery-planning-for-failure.md)).

## Planning for failure

**Hardware.** LND is very read and write intensive. It should not be run on an SD card, and SSDs are preferable to HDDs. Consider RAID, and if using a cloud provider choose one known for reliable hardware and uptime ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/recovery-planning-for-failure.md)).

**aezeed.** The 24-word seed generated by `lncli create` derives the node public key and the private keys of all on-chain funds. With it alone you can recover all on-chain funds and the funds from channels your peers force close after your node goes offline. Keep it on paper or in encrypted storage ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/recovery-planning-for-failure.md)).

**Static channel backups (SCB).** One SCB exists per channel; it holds information about the channel and peer and is encrypted with the node's private key, so it is only usable together with your aezeed. The `channel.backup` file holds the backups for all current channels. Export a single channel's SCB with `lncli exportchanbackup --chan_point <channel point>`. Keep a copy of `channel.backup` on a separate machine and update it whenever a new channel is opened ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/recovery-planning-for-failure.md)).

**Channel database.** `channel.db` cannot be recovered from directly, but information from it can help recover funds from channels whose peers are unreachable, using advanced tools. Back it up regularly but do not rely on it for recovery ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/recovery-planning-for-failure.md)). The secure-your-node guide adds that restoring an outdated `channel.db` may lose funds and that the file can only be used when migrating, not restoring ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/secure-your-lightning-network-node.md)).

**Zombie channels.** Close channels with peers you do not expect to come back online, as well as older channels without an SCB or with peers that do not support it. Do not confuse zombies with private channels to peers that only come online occasionally ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/recovery-planning-for-failure.md)).

## Disaster recovery

Use this only as a last resort, after examining logs, upgrading LND and analysing the problem. Following it closes all your channels ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/disaster-recovery.md)).

1. **Recover as much data as you can**: the aezeed (or the master key in `wallet.db` plus its password; the seed may also have a cipher seed passphrase that differs from the wallet password), the SCB (`channel.backup` or per-channel text strings), the most recent `channel.db` (only if recovered directly from the crashed node), and third-party information such as Lightning Network explorers. If possible, back up the entire `.lnd` directory ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/disaster-recovery.md)).
2. **Set up a node.** If reusing the same machine, move the old `.lnd` folder away so recovery starts with an empty directory. Otherwise keep the old node off during the whole process and beyond ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/disaster-recovery.md)).
3. **Initialize.** Run `lncli create`, choose a password of at least eight characters, and enter the old aezeed (and its cipher seed passphrase if encrypted). The node rescans the chain for on-chain funds. Check with `lncli walletbalance`; move coins out with `lncli sendcoins --sweepall <your bitcoin address>` ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/disaster-recovery.md)).
4. **Restore the SCB.** Wait until `lncli getinfo` shows the node synced to chain and graph, then run `lncli restorechanbackup --multi_file /path/to/file/channel.backup`, or per channel `lncli --single_backup <hex encoded channel backup>`. Your node asks former peers to force close; funds should arrive quickly and be spendable immediately. Peers may be offline, so it can be worth waiting a day or two, and connecting manually to peers that do not respond ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/disaster-recovery.md)).
5. **Force close the rest.** For peers that do not support SCB, are gone, or failed themselves, tools such as Chantools can publish commitment transactions from the channel database. This is only safe with a database recovered directly from the failed node; an outdated one risks losing all funds in those channels ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/disaster-recovery.md)).
6. **Manual intervention.** Contact channel partners directly and ask them to force close ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/disaster-recovery.md)).
7. **Secure your funds** by sweeping them to a separate wallet with `lncli sendcoins --sweepall <your bitcoin address>` ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/disaster-recovery.md)).

Additional tools listed: Guggero's Chantools, Telegram Cheeserobot, and the Zombie Channel Recovery Matcher ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/disaster-recovery.md)).

## Migrating to a new machine

Do NOT use the migration guide after a fatal error, do NOT run two LND instances with the same seed or public key, and do NOT restart an old instance after migration. Migrating between different operating systems or chip architectures is not advised ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/migrating-lnd.md)).

1. **Prepare the new machine**: update and harden it, install and sync a Bitcoin node (unless using Neutrino), install Tor if desired, install LND and any auxiliary services such as Litd, Loop, Pool or Faraday ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/migrating-lnd.md)).
2. **Stop the old node** with `lncli stop` and wait for it to shut down completely; never turn it on again ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/migrating-lnd.md)).
3. **Move the data.** Unless `lnd.conf` says otherwise, everything needed is in `~/.lnd`. Move it to the same location on the new machine, or point to it with `--lnddir=`. Logs and data can be split with `--datdir=` and `--logdir=` ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/migrating-lnd.md)).
4. **Migrate Litd data.** With funds in a Loop static address or Pool account, also migrate `~/.loop` and `~/.pool`; migrate `~/.lit` to keep LNC sessions and other data. This may be done separately from LND ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/migrating-lnd.md)).
5. **Check the configuration**: Bitcoin RPC host and credentials, Bitcoin ZMQ, Tor SOCKS proxy, control port and password, and whether a new IP, domain or onion needs a new TLS certificate ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/migrating-lnd.md)).
6. **Delete TLS certificates** and key in `~/.lnd` unless the address will not change, so they regenerate. Optionally delete the macaroon files together with `macaroon.db` in `~/.lnd/data/chain/bitcoin/mainnet` to invalidate old copies ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/migrating-lnd.md)).
7. **Start LND**, unlock the wallet, and verify with `lncli getinfo`, `lncli walletbalance` and `lncli channelbalance`. Then delete the lnd directory on the old platform ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/migrating-lnd.md)).

## Conflicts

- **Location of `channel.backup`.** Disaster recovery: `.lnd/data/chain/bitcoin/mainnet` ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/disaster-recovery.md)). Planning for failure: `.lnd/data/chain/bitcoin/channel.backup` ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/recovery-planning-for-failure.md)). Secure your node: `~/.lnd/data/chain/bitcoin/mainnet/channel.backup` ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/secure-your-lightning-network-node.md)).
- **Location of `channel.db`.** Disaster recovery: `.lnd/data/graph/mainnet` ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/disaster-recovery.md)). Planning for failure: `.lnd/graph/mainnet` ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/recovery-planning-for-failure.md)).
- **When `channel.backup` changes.** Planning for failure: updated every time a new channel is opened ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/recovery-planning-for-failure.md)). Secure your node: changed every time a channel is opened or closed ([source](../../raw/docs/lightning-network-tools/lnd/secure-your-lightning-network-node.md)).
